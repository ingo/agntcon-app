Your conference, in your pocket.

See what is on now, plan your days on a calendar that spots clashes, keep private notes on sessions and speakers, remember the people you met, and take it all home as a trip report. It reads the public Sessionize schedule, so it works for any event that publishes there.

Made for the way you use a conference app

Between sessions you want three answers fast: where do I need to be, what is on while I am free, and who was that person I just talked to. This app is built around those, and everything personal stays on your phone.

What it does

Now

What you are in and how long is left, what is next and how soon, its room and floor, and the free time before it, with a button to browse what starts then.

A calendar that keeps up

Your bookmarks on a timeline. Overlaps are marked as clashes, free time is split at the conference's own breaks, a red line shows the time, sessions that are over fade back and the one you are in is highlighted.

Search that looks inside

Find sessions by title, speaker, description or company. Filter by topic, level, format or company.

Speakers and companies

Browse speakers with their LinkedIn, blog and company links. A Companies tab lists who is here from where, and a Solutions Showcase filter lets you browse exhibitor talks by company.

Private notes and ratings

Jot a note on any session or speaker. Give a session a thumbs up or down and a one-line takeaway. All of it is stored on your device only.

People I met

Mark a speaker as met, add a note, and find them again later in one list with their LinkedIn link, ready for the follow-up.

Take it with you

Add a session or your whole schedule to your calendar, share it as text, or export a Markdown trip report with your sessions, ratings, notes, slides and the people you met.

Find the room

A venue map with a pin per room. Tap "Find on map" on a session, or the pin on a schedule row, to see where it is.

Stays in step with Sessionize

Link the app to the event's Sessionize web app with a six-digit code and your bookmarks stay the same in both places. Slides and recordings appear on the session page when speakers publish them.

See it

Your notes are yours

Notes, ratings and the list of people you met are saved on your device, per event, and the app does not send them anywhere. The schedule itself comes from the event's public Sessionize feed. The optional bookmark sync talks to the event's Sessionize web app, and only when you link it.

The app is open source, so you can read exactly what it does.

For any event on Sessionize

A conference needs no server of its own: add a profile with its Sessionize id, time zone and colors, and build. The schedule, speakers, topics and levels come from the feed. The README shows how.
